## Observation And Hypothesis

The learner correctly identified the equipment-based causal explanation as a
hypothesis. He proposed examining the relationship between room equipment and
booking choices, including surveying users about their reasons. He independently
suggested proximity to a coffee machine as an alternative explanation. This
demonstrates seeking additional evidence and considering competing explanations.

evidence for one does not automatically falsify the other. These refinements
extend the response; they do not negate his correct hypothesis identification
or useful proposal to collect evidence.
